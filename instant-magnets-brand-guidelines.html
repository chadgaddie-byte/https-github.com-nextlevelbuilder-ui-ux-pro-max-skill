01 — The Refresh

Same identity.
Sharper execution.

The existing Instant Magnets mark already works — a strong charcoal “IM”, the stacked INSTANT / MAGNETS panels, and that friendly signature blue. This refresh keeps all of it. What changes is the finish: a cleaner blue with a touch more life, slightly warmer charcoal, refined letter-spacing that matches the logo’s own rhythm, and a full system around it so the brand looks just as considered on a website, an invoice, or a magnet as it does on the logo.

04 / Typography
04 — Typography

Geometric & clean.

Jost echoes the geometric, wide-set capitals already in your logo — it’s the natural type voice for the brand. Outfit handles body copy and UI: friendly, legible, modern. Together they keep everything looking effortless and current.

Outfit carries all body copy, captions, buttons and fine print. It sits comfortably beneath Jost without competing — warm, rounded and highly legible at small sizes. Build hierarchy with weight and space rather than colour, and keep line lengths comfortable for easy reading.

06 / Voice
06 — Tone of Voice

Warm host, not a salesperson.

We talk like the friendly face at the event — upbeat, easy, never pushy. Short sentences, real warmth, and we let the keepsake do the bragging.

We are

Friendly · Effortless · Modern

  • “Your favourite moment, ready before the party ends.”
  • “Printed and made right there, on-site.”
  • “Pick a frame — we’ll handle the rest.”
  • Confident, generous, lightly playful.
We’re not

Loud · Salesy · Stiff

  • “BEST MAGNETS!!! ORDER NOW!!!”
  • “Cheapest prices guaranteed, don’t miss out.”
  • “Utilising premium magnetic substrate solutions.”
  • Pushy, jargon-heavy or impersonal.
