How it works

Snap. Print. Keep.

Three easy steps — and your guests leave with something they'll actually keep.

  1. 1

    We arrive & set up

    Our team turns up before the event begins and sets up a tidy little photo station — no fuss, no big footprint.

  2. 2

    Guests get photographed

    We capture candid moments and posed portraits through the night. Pick a frame — we'll handle the rest.

  3. 3

    Magnets, printed on-site

    Each photo becomes a custom fridge magnet in minutes — printed and made right there, ready before the party ends.
